About

Santi Fernández Andrés

Hey there! I'm Santi, a video-game designer and programmer, mainly in Godot and Unity. With almost 3 years of work experience and a multidisciplinary profile, to aid covering other tasks in small projects or to be a helping hand for my teammates.

I'm used to learning about engines and their tools, as well as translating the essence of design into a programming language, operating from level design to system implementation. Also, I can make shaders and I'm good at solving software problems.

When I'm free, I enjoy participating in gamejams with friends. And the last years I've been diving into the world of Gnu/Linux and open source software, seeking for digital sovereignty and helping others to take the leap.

Most of the projects I've been working on are on this web. If you like what you see, don't doubt talking with me about working together.

Let's work together!
